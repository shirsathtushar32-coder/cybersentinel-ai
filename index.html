TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

How it works

A simple guide to the workflow and the limits of the current prototype.

01

Provide log text

Paste a relevant excerpt or use fictional demo samples. The app reads the text you provide.

02

Match local rules

Transparent pattern checks look for common indicators such as repeated failures, suspicious query strings, and traversal patterns.

03

Review risk estimate

Matched indicators contribute to a weighted score. This estimate is a triage aid, not a validated probability.

04

Document response

Track a checklist, write case notes, and export a plain-text summary for further review.

CURRENT LIMITATIONS

What this prototype does not do

  • Does not monitor a computer or network live.
  • Does not contact an AI service or external API.
  • Does not authenticate users or store cases in a shared database.
  • Does not automatically block accounts, delete files, or isolate devices.
  • Does not prove that an incident occurred.

Project objectives

  • Practice interpreting suspicious log patterns.
  • Summarize findings and risk indicators.
  • Support structured incident response learning.
  • Document observations and evidence context.

Privacy & responsible use

This version performs analysis in the browser and contains no remote log-upload feature. Use sanitized examples where possible. Browser storage is not encrypted and may be cleared by the browser, so do not enter secrets or sensitive production data.
